so question for you today:

what is our calling? what is our vision, and who are we to be?

my vision is to build you up to the fulness to that which Christ calls you,

so i want to support you in your vision and your calling,

and today we’re going to look at what that is! im not going to push a vision on you

lets look at what the Bible says:

in Mark chapter1,

we have the calling of the disciples, -- they read

v16-17 As Jesus was walking beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw Simon and his brother Andrew. They were casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen. 17“Come, follow Me,” Jesus said, “and I will make you fishers of men”

Jesus calls us to be fishers of men, or elsewhere he calls us as the light of the world (matt5.14) % let my little light shine

the smallest, tinyiest things are precious and valuable to the LORD

so i would say, Jesus is not saying He is going to be the church, but that He is going to build us up, to be the light of the world

so i want to do that, to support you, and build you up into your calling

but there is a calling before that, the calling of Jesus, this is somehow the first vision

v10-11 And a voice came from heaven: “You are my Son, whom I love; with you I am well pleased”

i would say, this our first calling

read it again

what does that mean for us?

.... peace, contentment, already pleased the Father, not needing punishment, but needing the embrace of one who welcomes the prodigals

this is our foundation, without this, no other calling matters

which is why we have been building family and relationship together, our identity is not teacher, or student, but son and beloved

nothing else matters – i dont come to teach, i come to be family

okay thats the beginning of a vision or calling
